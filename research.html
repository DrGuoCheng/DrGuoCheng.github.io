Research

Efficient and Trustworthy Perception

Methods for streaming visual and multimodal intelligence in autonomous systems

Sequential and Streaming Vision

Visual inputs from cameras and other sensors arrive continuously, yet conventional models often repeat computation across highly related spatial regions and consecutive frames. This research develops sequential inference methods that exploit structure and continuity while preserving useful prediction quality.

  • Sequential Semantic Segmentation (SE3)
  • Streaming image and video understanding
  • Spatial and temporal computation reuse
  • Motion-aware representation and inference
  • Sequential CNN and Transformer architectures

Efficient and Deployable AI

Autonomous systems operate under real constraints on latency, memory, compute, energy, and communication. This direction studies learning and inference methods that translate algorithmic efficiency into practical system performance.

  • Lightweight and hardware-aware models
  • Memory-efficient and low-latency inference
  • Adaptive computation and resource allocation
  • Edge intelligence and real-time deployment
  • Evaluation of accuracy-efficiency tradeoffs

Trustworthy Multimodal Intelligence

Reliable autonomous intelligence requires more than high average accuracy. This direction studies how visual, linguistic, and sensor information can be integrated and evaluated when observations are incomplete, ambiguous, shifted, or otherwise challenging.

  • Vision-language and multimodal learning
  • Robustness and uncertainty
  • Semantic alignment and failure analysis
  • Reliable sensor and information fusion
  • Trustworthy perception for safety-critical systems

Autonomous and Intelligent Systems

Application areas include autonomous vehicles, intelligent transportation, robotics, unmanned and maritime systems, and connected edge platforms. The long-term goal is to connect efficient perception with reliable decision support and action in dynamic environments.
