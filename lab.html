About

The FAST Vision Lab develops efficient and trustworthy visual and multimodal intelligence for autonomous systems. We study how intelligent systems can process streaming data under practical constraints on computation, memory, latency, communication, uncertainty, and reliability.

Research

🎞️

Sequential and Streaming Vision

Sequential semantic segmentation, streaming video understanding, motion-aware perception, temporal modeling, and computation reuse across space and time.

⚡

Efficient and Deployable AI

Fast and lightweight models, memory-efficient inference, hardware-aware learning, adaptive computation, and real-time deployment on autonomous and edge platforms.

🛡️

Trustworthy Multimodal Intelligence

Reliable vision-language and multimodal models, robustness, uncertainty, semantic alignment, failure analysis, and trustworthy perception in dynamic environments.

Application Areas

Autonomous vehicles, intelligent transportation, robotics, unmanned and maritime systems, connected sensing, and other safety-critical applications.

Team

Guo Cheng

Director · Assistant Professor of Computer Science

Guo Cheng directs the FAST Vision Lab at TAMU-CC. His research focuses on efficient and trustworthy perception for autonomous systems, including sequential visual inference, streaming scene understanding, and multimodal intelligence.
