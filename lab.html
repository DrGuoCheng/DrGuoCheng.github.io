Research Group

FAST Vision Lab

Fast, Adaptive, Sequential, and Trustworthy Vision for Autonomous and Intelligent Systems

Directed by Dr. Guo Cheng at Texas A&M University–Corpus Christi

Mission

The FAST Vision Lab develops efficient and trustworthy visual and multimodal intelligence for autonomous systems. We investigate how intelligent systems can process streaming data under practical constraints on computation, memory, latency, communication, uncertainty, and reliability.

Main Research Directions

01

Sequential and Streaming Vision

Sequential semantic segmentation, streaming video understanding, motion-aware perception, temporal modeling, and computation reuse across space and time.

02

Efficient and Deployable AI

Fast and lightweight models, memory-efficient inference, hardware-aware learning, adaptive computation, and real-time deployment on vehicles, robots, and edge platforms.

03

Trustworthy Multimodal Intelligence

Reliable vision-language and multimodal models, robustness, uncertainty, semantic alignment, failure analysis, and trustworthy perception in dynamic environments.

Application Areas

Our methods support autonomous vehicles, intelligent transportation, robotics, unmanned and maritime systems, connected sensing, and other safety-critical applications.
