Prospective Ph.D. Students

I am recruiting Ph.D. students to begin in Fall 2027. The research will focus on efficient and trustworthy perception for autonomous systems. Admission and financial support are subject to university requirements and final approval.

Research Directions

  • Efficient sequential perception: streaming video understanding, sequential semantic segmentation, computation reuse, and memory- and latency-efficient CNN and Transformer inference.
  • Trustworthy multimodal perception: vision-language model reliability, robustness under perceptual degradation, uncertainty, and semantic alignment.
  • Autonomous and edge intelligence: real-time perception for autonomous vehicles, robotics, connected systems, and other resource-constrained platforms.

What I Look For

  • Strong motivation to conduct research in computer vision, machine learning, multimodal intelligence, autonomous systems, or a closely related area.
  • Solid preparation in Python, data structures, linear algebra, probability, statistics, and calculus.
  • Experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow and evidence of completing a substantial technical or research project.
  • Persistence, intellectual curiosity, careful experimental practice, and willingness to improve research writing and presentation.
  • A bachelor's or master's degree in computer science, electrical or computer engineering, mathematics, statistics, or a related field.

Research Training

Students will receive structured mentoring in research problem formulation, literature review, experimental design, reproducible implementation, academic writing, presentation, and professional development. My advising emphasizes clear milestones, rigorous experimentation, honest communication, and increasing research independence.

About TAMU-CC

Texas A&M University–Corpus Christi is a member of the Texas A&M University System. Its Department of Computer Science, within the College of Engineering and Computer Science, offers doctoral study in Computer Science. The university's work in autonomous, coastal, marine, and unmanned systems creates opportunities for applied computer-vision and intelligent-systems research.

How to Contact Me

Email guo.cheng@tamucc.edu with the subject:

Please include:

  • A concise description of your research interests and their alignment with my group.

  • One representative writing sample, such as a paper, thesis chapter, research report, or substantial course-project report.
  • Links to research code or technical projects, if available.

Contacting me does not replace the university application. Prospective students must satisfy all university and program requirements and submit an official application to the TAMU-CC Computer Science Ph.D. program.
