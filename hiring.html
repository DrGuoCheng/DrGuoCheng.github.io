Ph.D. Opportunity at TAMU-CC

I am recruiting one Ph.D. student to begin in Fall 2027. The position will focus on efficient and trustworthy perception for autonomous systems. Additional openings may become available subject to funding. Funding and admission are subject to university requirements and final approval.

Research directions

  • Sequential and efficient perception: streaming video understanding, semantic segmentation, detection, tracking, computation reuse, and memory-efficient CNN and Transformer inference.
  • Trustworthy multimodal perception: vision-language model reliability, robustness under perceptual degradation, calibration, uncertainty, and semantic alignment.
  • Edge and autonomous intelligence: hardware-aware inference and real-time perception for autonomous vehicles, robotics, connected systems, and unmanned platforms.

What I provide as an advisor

  • Structured research training in problem formulation, literature review, experimental design, reproducible implementation, academic writing, and presentation.
  • Regular mentorship with clear milestones, constructive feedback, and increasing research independence.
  • Research computing resources appropriate for deep-learning and autonomous-perception projects.
  • Engineering rigor informed by my experience at Bloomberg L.P., including reproducible code, version control, scalable experimentation, and deployment awareness.
  • Professional development supporting conference participation, collaborations, internship applications, award nominations, and academic or industry career preparation.
  • Use-inspired research opportunities. During my doctoral research, I contributed to projects supported by the U.S. Department of Transportation and the Toyota Collaborative Safety Research Center.

What I expect

  • Responsibility, persistence, and self-motivation. Research involves uncertainty, iteration, and occasional failure.
  • Intellectual curiosity. You should want to understand why a method works, not only how to run existing code.
  • Technical preparation. Solid foundations in Python, linear algebra, probability, statistics, and calculus are expected; C++ is useful but not required.
  • Machine-learning experience. Familiarity with PyTorch or TensorFlow and evidence of completing a substantial technical project are strongly preferred.
  • Research communication. You should be willing to read papers critically, document experiments carefully, discuss difficulties openly, and improve your writing.
  • Relevant degree. Applicants should hold a bachelor's or master's degree in computer science, electrical or computer engineering, mathematics, statistics, or a related discipline.

About TAMU-CC

  • Texas A&M University-Corpus Christi is a member of the Texas A&M University System and is located on the Texas Gulf Coast.
  • The Department of Computer Science is part of the College of Engineering and Computer Science and offers doctoral study in Computer Science.
  • The university's strengths in autonomous, coastal, marine, and unmanned systems create opportunities for applied computer-vision and intelligent-systems research.

How to contact me

Email guo.cheng@tamucc.edu with the subject line:

Prospective Ph.D. Student - Fall 2027 - [Your Name]

Please include the following in one message:

  • A concise description of your research interests and why they align with my group.
  • Your CV or resume.
  • Unofficial undergraduate and graduate transcripts.
  • One representative writing sample, such as a paper, thesis chapter, research report, or substantial course-project report.
  • Links to research code or technical projects, if available.

Contacting me does not replace the formal application. Prospective students must also satisfy all university and program requirements and submit an official application to the TAMU-CC Computer Science Ph.D. program. Because of message volume, I may not be able to reply individually to every inquiry.
