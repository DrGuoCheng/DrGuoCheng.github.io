Publications

My research focuses on efficient and trustworthy perception for autonomous systems. My name is shown in bold. See also Google Scholar and GitHub.

Selected contributions

Sequential Semantic Segmentation

Introduced sequential inference methods for large images and streaming driving scenes, reducing memory and redundant computation while supporting planning-oriented perception.

SE3 at ICPR 2022 · IEEE T-ITS 2022

Trustworthy Vision-Language Models

Studies semantic misalignment in vision-language models when visual evidence is degraded.

arXiv:2601.08355

Robust Driving Perception

Developed compact road representations and video-mining methods for adverse weather, illumination, efficient sensing, and autonomous-driving planning.
