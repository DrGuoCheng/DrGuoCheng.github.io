👋 I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Texas A&M University–Corpus Christi, where I direct the FAST Vision Lab. Before joining TAMU-CC, I worked as a software engineer at Bloomberg L.P.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Purdue University in 2022, advised by Professor Jiangyu Zheng. My research focuses on efficient and trustworthy perception for autonomous systems.

RESEARCH — I develop methods for processing streaming visual and multimodal data efficiently and reliably, with applications to autonomous vehicles, robotics, and edge intelligence. Research directions.
