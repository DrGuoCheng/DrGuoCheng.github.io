About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Texas A&M University–Corpus Christi, where I direct the FAST Vision Lab. My research focuses on efficient and trustworthy perception for autonomous systems.

I develop methods for processing streaming visual and multimodal data efficiently and reliably, with applications to autonomous vehicles, robotics, and edge intelligence.

Before joining TAMU-CC, I worked as a software engineer at Bloomberg L.P. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Purdue University in 2022, advised by Professor Jiangyu Zheng. My work has appeared in IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles, ICPR, IEEE IV, and ITSC.

FAST Vision Lab

The FAST Vision Lab studies Fast, Adaptive, Sequential, and Trustworthy Vision. We build efficient visual and multimodal intelligence that can operate reliably in dynamic, resource-constrained environments.
